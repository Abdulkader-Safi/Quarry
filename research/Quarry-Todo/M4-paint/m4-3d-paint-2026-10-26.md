# M4 · Paint on the model in the 3D viewport

FR-PAINT-02. This is the reason painting exists next to the 2D panel.

**Build**
- Texel picking from the renderer: cursor → face → UV → texel, respecting the pixel grid.
- Stroke continues across a UV seam so a line across two faces is continuous.
- Brush size in pixels, colour from the palette, pressure hook from M0.
- One undo entry per stroke; painted texels visible immediately without a full texture reload.

**Done when**
- [x] Paint a line across two faces sharing a seam: continuous in 3D.
- [ ] Zoomed out, the stroke does not bleed onto back-facing faces.
- [x] Undo removes the whole stroke.

## Backend done 2026-10-27 (UI waits for the Figma design)

- `paint.stroke_3d(points, rgba, size?, pressures?)` walks the 3D path in quarter-texel steps, finds the closest face point (local space, barycentric UV) and stamps that texel, so a line crossing a seam has no gap. One undo step.
- Found and fixed on the way: `uv.unwrap` could lay pieces outside a small texture (a box net is 48x64 px at 1 m = 16 px); it now shrinks the layout to fit the faces' image.
- Tested: a stroke over the top face, across the edge and down the front paints inside both faces' UV areas; undo removes it.
- Left: viewport texel picking from the renderer (cursor to face to texel), back-face rejection, live texture updates.

**UI (2026-10-26)**
UI: pencil in the view sends paint.stroke_3d along the picked surface points.
