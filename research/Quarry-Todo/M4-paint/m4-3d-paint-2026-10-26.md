# M4 · Paint on the model in the 3D viewport

FR-PAINT-02. This is the reason painting exists next to the 2D panel.

**Build**
- Texel picking from the renderer: cursor → face → UV → texel, respecting the pixel grid.
- Stroke continues across a UV seam so a line across two faces is continuous.
- Brush size in pixels, colour from the palette, pressure hook from M0.
- One undo entry per stroke; painted texels visible immediately without a full texture reload.

**Done when**
- [ ] Paint a line across two faces sharing a seam: continuous in 3D.
- [ ] Zoomed out, the stroke does not bleed onto back-facing faces.
- [ ] Undo removes the whole stroke.
