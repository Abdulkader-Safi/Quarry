# M4 · Optional: vertex colours and AO bake

**Checklist**
- [x] FR-PAINT-05 vertex colours paint per vertex, multiply over the texture, with a view toggle.
- [x] `paint.bake_ao` into vertex colours, long-running with progress and cancel (uses the M1 progress plumbing).

## Done 2026-10-27

- `paint.vertex_color(rgba, points?, radius?)`: colours every corner on the vertices near a stroke (world points) or on the selection. The Vertex colour tool in Paint now sends strokes here (radius 0.1 m per brush px) instead of painting the texture.
- View toggle: `view.display vertex_color` now hides them (white) in the viewport; on by default. View menu: Toggle vertex colours. Colours multiply over the texture and export as COLOR_0 as before.
- `paint.bake_ao(rays?, distance?, strength?)`: hemisphere rays from each vertex against every visible mesh, multiplied into the vertex colours. Long command: reports progress via `cx.step` and stops on `control.cancel` with nothing changed. Brute force (every ray against every triangle), fine for low-poly; a BVH if scenes grow. Paint panel: Bake AO into vertex colours.
- Tests: painting a vertex sets exactly its corners; a box on a floor bakes darker at the bottom than the top.
- Also fixed: the Paint panel's Pen pressure checkbox now gates pen pressure.
- For review: the window was not capturable for a screenshot at the time (macOS refused every window capture), so the look of a bake is unchecked. In the app the bake runs on the UI thread; cancel is reachable from MCP only.
