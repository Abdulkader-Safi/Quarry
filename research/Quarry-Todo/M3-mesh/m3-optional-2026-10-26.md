## 2026-10-27

- FR-SCENE-06 was already in: `scene.set_props` sets key-values on any object and the export writes them under `extras.quarry.props` (checked in `export.rs`, `a_tiled_room_keeps_nearest_filtering_names_and_extras`).
- FR-MESH-07 (new `commands/meshtools.rs`): `mesh.inset(amount)`, `mesh.subdivide`, `mesh.bevel(edge?, width?)` (one segment, ends touching at most three faces), `mesh.knife(from?, to?)` (points on two edges of a face; defaults to the middles of two selected edges) and `select.loop(edge?)`. Faces stay triangles or quads: 5- and 6-corner pieces are split. In the Mesh menu and the palette. Tests: inset ring, subdivided box is 24 quads with shared midpoints and an 8-edge middle loop, bevel stays watertight with consistent winding, knife splits the face and its neighbours.
- FR-MESH-09: `mesh.shade(mode: flat|smooth|auto, angle?, object?)`. Auto flood-fills smoothing groups across edges gentler than the angle (default 40°), so a cylinder's sides blend and its caps stay hard (test). `Mesh::corner_normals` is now shared by the viewport (which drew everything flat before) and the glTF export. The Object panel's Smooth shading checkbox runs auto/flat; Mesh menu has Shade smooth and Shade flat. Checked by screenshot: flat and smooth 16-sided cylinders side by side.
- FR-UV-03: `uv.pack(padding?)` finds islands from the current UVs (faces joined where their UVs match along an edge) and shelf-packs them inside the faces' image. When shrinking to fit it packs again with wider gaps, so the padding survives the shrink; `uv.unwrap` uses the same layout (before, its shrink also shrank the 1 px gap). Test: 24 islands of a seamed cylinder in 64 px, all inside and at least 1 px apart. The UVs panel's Pack islands button (which ran unwrap) now runs pack with the Padding field.
- FR-SCENE-07: `scene.duplicate(objects?, mirror?, center?)`. Mirrored mesh copies get the flip baked into their mesh (winding reversed) so their transform keeps positive scale; mirrored instances get a negative scale, and the viewport now winds faces back for any object whose transform mirrors. `.L`/`.R` swap in names (bones already had `rig.mirror_bones`). Mirrored skinned copies come back unbound with a warning. Edit menu: Duplicate (⇧D) and Duplicate mirrored on X. Test: a rotated wedge Arm.L mirrors to Arm.R, an exact mirror image with outward faces.
- All five boxes ticked; review: try the new mesh tools and mirrored duplicates by hand.
