**Build**
- `rig.skeleton_new`; `rig.bone_add` at the crosshair, chained off the selected bone (head = parent tail) or branched.
- `rig.bone_edit`: name, parent, roll, head/tail drag; `rig.bone_delete` reparents or clears children explicitly.
- Show/hide bones, bone display size, max 128 bones per skeleton.
- Name uniqueness enforced; `.L`/`.R` conventions suggested.
