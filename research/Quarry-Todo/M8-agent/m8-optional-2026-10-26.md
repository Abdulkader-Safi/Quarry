# M8 · Optional: high-level auto-rig helpers

**Checklist**
- [x] FR-MCP-10 `rig.biped_auto` on a humanoid makes a named 15-bone skeleton bound with weights.
- [x] Quadruped variant; a walk-cycle starter clip.
- [x] All built on the low-level commands, so a human can do the same steps by hand.

## Done 2026-10-27

- `rig.biped_auto(object, name?, walk?)`: 15 bones fitted to the mesh's bounds (hips, spine, chest, neck, head; upper_arm, lower_arm, hand, thigh, shin .L/.R), arms in a T when the mesh is wide else down its sides, knees bent slightly forward, leg IK with poles in front, automatic weights. `walk: true` adds a looping "walk" clip keying the feet's IK targets and swinging the arms.
- `rig.quadruped_auto(object, name?, walk?)`: 14 bones along the longer horizontal axis (hips, spine, chest, neck, head, tail; front/back upper and lower .L/.R), front knees back and hind knees forward, IK on all four, weights; the walk moves diagonal pairs together.
- Both replay ordinary commands (`rig.skeleton_new`, `rig.bone_add`, `rig.ik_add`, `rig.bind_auto`, `anim.clip_new`, `anim.key_set`) from inside one command, so they are one undo step and a person can repeat the steps by hand. Rig menu: Auto-rig biped / quadruped with walk. The agent guide mentions them.
- Test: a 1.8 m box gets 15 bones with the head tail at its top, is bound, walks, and one undo removes the rig and clip; a long box gets 14 bones and 4 IK chains.
- For review: the fit on a real character mesh (a box only proves the plumbing).
