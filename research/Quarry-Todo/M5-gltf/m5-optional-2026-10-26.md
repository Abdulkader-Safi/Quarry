## Done 2026-10-27

- FR-EXP-06: `export.preset_save(name, path, format?, selection?, objects?, scale?)`, `export.preset_run(name?)` (default the newest) and `export.preset_delete(name)`. Presets live in the project (`export_presets`), with paths inside the project folder kept relative. The Export dialog saves its settings as a preset after each export ("Remember these settings for Export again", on by default) and ⇧⌘E (Export again, rebindable) re-runs the newest. Test: saved relative, re-exported, still there after save and reopen.
- FR-EXP-08: `export.run format obj` writes `.obj` + `.mtl` + one PNG per used image beside it: world-space positions (with the export scale), per-corner UVs (v flipped for OBJ) and normals (smooth groups honoured), one material per image, mirrored objects wound back. The Export dialog has a Format choice (.glb or .obj). Test: a textured box gives 8 v, 24 vt, 6 f, a material pointing at a 16x16 PNG.
