## Progress 2026-10-27

- FR-ANIM-07 onion skin: `view.display onion` (saved with the project). With a clip shown, the viewport draws dim edge outlines of every mesh skinned to its skeleton, posed at the key before the playhead (warm) and the key after (cool). The timeline's Onion skin checkbox, which did nothing before, now drives it (off by default). Test: a swinging box at 0.5 s adds its 12 edges at each neighbouring key.
- FR-ANIM-09: `anim.pose_mirror` flips the current pose; `anim.clip_mirror(clip?, name?)` makes a mirrored copy, keyed at the original key times (IK solved into rotations, linear between keys). Mirroring works in skeleton space from each bone's twin, so bone rolls do not matter as long as the rest pose is symmetric. Animate menu has both. Test: an arm.L pose lands on arm.R as its exact mirror, for the pose and for a mirrored clip.
